How It Works

From request to invoice, without leaving Bidmana

Engaging a consultant is a few clicks, and the administrative side is handled by the platform so your team can stay focused on the bid.

Find the right person

Browse consultant profiles by skills, programs, and agencies they have worked, with a professional bio and an hourly rate on every card. Engage directly from the card, and the consultant joins your workspace with access scoped to the pursuits you choose.

Or post a job

Post the help you need and let registered consultant firms bid. Accepting a bid starts the engagement.

Work together, in one place

Shared pipeline, proposal workspace, client documents, messaging, and calendar. Consultants log hours against the engagement with a memo for every entry.

Approve hours, pay once

Review and approve time entries, or flag them with a note. Approved hours become a numbered invoice you can pay through Bidmana, with nothing to reconcile afterward.

For Consultants

Bring your expertise to companies that need it

Independent GovCon professionals and consulting firms can join Bidmana as consultants. Build a profile from your resume, set your rate, and take on engagements with companies already working inside the platform.

You get your own workspace, a view into each client's pursuits while you act on their behalf, and a clean record of hours and invoices for every engagement.

A profile that sells your experience

Upload a resume and Bidmana drafts a professional bio, extracts skills and programs, and lists the agencies you know.

Open jobs and direct engagements

Bid on jobs that companies post, or be engaged directly from your card by a company that found you.

Time tracking and payouts

Log hours with memos, see approvals and flags in one place, and receive payment through Bidmana once the client approves.

Independent by design

Every consultant certifies non-collusion at signup, and each engagement is visible to both sides, so companies can trust who is working for whom.
