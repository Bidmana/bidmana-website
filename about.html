About Bidmana

Built by people who have lived the work

Bidmana is built by veterans who have run captures, written proposals, secured federal systems, and delivered on government contracts from the inside. We built the platform we wished we had.

Leadership

Meet the leadership team

Three veterans with complementary backgrounds in federal capture, cybersecurity and systems architecture, and large-scale cloud and software delivery.

Richard Kim

Co-Founder, CEO

Richard brings over a decade of Federal contracting expertise spanning the Department of Defense, civilian agencies, and the private sector. Throughout his career, he has led capture efforts, proposal management, contract administration, and business development initiatives supporting millions of dollars in Federal opportunities. He is a proud U.S. Air Force veteran and continues to serve in the Air Force Reserve.

Greg Trent

Co-Founder, CTO

Greg leads the company's technical vision and is a proud U.S. Army veteran. He brings a background in cybersecurity, DevSecOps, federal systems architecture, and defense program execution, giving Bidmana a technical foundation grounded in the realities of government contracting, compliance-heavy environments, and mission-focused delivery.

Shane Vaughn

Chief Information Officer

Shane is a retired U.S. Air Force veteran with more than 25 years of combined military and government contracting experience. His industry background spans storage, software development, networking, cloud, security, and DevSecOps. Shane leads Bidmana's cloud and software development, bringing the discipline of large-scale government systems to how the platform is built, secured, and operated.
