What You Get

Six views of your market

Each view is built from your company profile, so the contracts, competitors, and agencies it surfaces are the ones in your NAICS codes and your set-aside categories, not a generic feed.

Recompete Radar

Contracts in your space that are approaching their end date, who holds them, how the agency has bought before, and how often task orders are issued. Signals from new RFPs are folded in as they appear.

Emerging Companies

New entrants winning work in your NAICS codes, with business size, certifications, and the awards that put them on the map. Filter by size to watch small businesses or the large primes moving into your lane.

Competitor Landscape and Black Hat briefs

Who you keep running into, where they win, and what they are likely to propose. Generate a Black Hat brief for a specific pursuit to pressure-test your own strategy.

Award history from USASpending and SAM.gov

Federal award records and SAM.gov award notices searchable by agency, NAICS, PSC, and vendor, with dollar ranges attached to live opportunities so you know what the work is worth.

Calibrated win probability

A pWin estimate for each pursuit that is calibrated against your own past outcomes, so the number means what it says and improves as you record wins and losses.

Analytics and scheduled reports

Win rate, pipeline health, and market movement on one dashboard, with intelligence reports emailed to your team as PDF on the schedule you choose.

The Technology

AI built for government contracting, not generic content

Bidmana's intelligence layer is purpose built for the GovCon domain. Every model, every feedback loop, and every data source is designed around how federal proposals actually work.

Grounded in real procurement data

Federal solicitations, agency buying patterns, award records, and your own proposal history feed the models. The system understands the language, structure, and expectations of government acquisition in a way generic AI cannot.

Reasoning the way a proposal professional does

Bidmana reasons about compliance, evaluation criteria, section structure, and competitive positioning. Every draft claim carries a citation, and a verifier flags anything it cannot support from your evidence.

Learns from your outcomes

Won and lost pursuits, review cycles, and compliance checks feed back into how Bidmana drafts and scores your next bid. It gets sharper with every engagement, guided by the GovCon professionals embedded in the platform.

Your knowledge stays yours

Your institutional knowledge is used only to make your own workspace sharper. It is never shared, never pooled with other customers, and never used to train models outside your organization. All AI runs on Vertex AI inside Bidmana's Assured Workloads boundary.
