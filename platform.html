The Platform

One workspace from opportunity to submission

Bidmana brings opportunity discovery, capture, proposal development, and your document library into a single system, so your team stops re-keying the same information across six tools.

Request a Demo
Opportunities

Every market, matched to you

Bidmana pulls federal, state and local, small business research, and federal grant opportunities into one feed and ranks them against your company profile: the NAICS codes you work in, the set-asides you qualify for, and the capabilities in your own past performance.

When you add a pursuit to your pipeline, Bidmana collects the solicitation package for you: the base notice, every amendment, the attachments, and the documents linked from the notice page.

Federal notices

Presolicitations, solicitations, sources sought, and awards, refreshed continuously, with alternate contacts and award-value benchmarks.

State and local bids in all 50 states and DC

State and local opportunities arrive in the same feed, with the same filters, as federal work.

SBIR and STTR topics

Open SBIR and STTR solicitations, including Department of Defense topics, with Phase I and Phase II filters.

Federal grants

Every open and forecast federal grant and cooperative agreement, narrowed to the ones your business is eligible to apply for, with the award range the agency published.

Filters that reflect how you bid

Eligible-only by default, active or closed, sole-source, deadline sort, award estimates, and state selection for SLED work.

Pipeline & Capture

Run capture like a program

Each pursuit gets a card with a capture manager, proposal writers, reviewers, and a business development team. The card owns the decision, the schedule, the contacts, and eventually the outcome.

Win or lose, the outcome feeds back into how Bidmana drafts your next proposal.

Amendment impact analysis

When an amendment drops, Bidmana reads it against the base solicitation and tells you what changed and which sections it touches.

Price to win

Build a target competitive price from award history, pricing notes, and competitive intelligence, then track it against your bid.

Calendar and meetings

Deadlines and reviews on a shared calendar, connected to your Outlook or Google account, with Teams meetings created from inside the platform.

Win strategy and bid decisions

Bid or no-bid frameworks, win themes, and a calibrated win probability so the decision is made on evidence, not instinct.

Proposal Workspace

Drafts grounded in your evidence

Bidmana does not write generic prose. It shreds the solicitation into requirements, builds the compliance matrix, and drafts each section from your own past performance and capability documents, with a citation behind every claim it makes.

A verifier reads the draft back against the evidence and flags anything unsupported before a reviewer ever sees it. The result reads like your company wrote it, because it did.

RFP shred and compliance matrix

Requirements, evaluation criteria, and pricing instructions extracted from Sections L and M into a matrix your team can assign and track.

Cited drafting with a verifier

Every section is drafted from an evidence pack built from your library. Unsupported claims are flagged and evidence gaps are listed for the reviewer.

PWS alignment and color reviews

Check each volume against the performance work statement, run structured Red Team reviews, and export a clean Word document when it is ready.

Your house style, not AI style

Bidmana learns your company's voice and scrubs the patterns readers associate with machine-written text, so evaluators read a proposal, not a prompt.

Portfolio

Your documents stay where they live

Bidmana connects to the document store you already use instead of becoming another copy of it. Point it at your past performance folders, and it reads them on demand to ground every draft. Point it at a solicitations folder, and every package it collects lands there, organized by pursuit.

Proposal files are never stored on Bidmana. If you disconnect a source, Bidmana forgets it.

SharePoint and OneDrive

Native Microsoft 365 connection. Source folders are re-read every few minutes, and renames follow automatically.

Google Drive and local folders

Pick files from Google Drive, or use a folder on your own computer or synced drive as the destination for packages.

Solicitation packages, filed for you

Base notice, amendments, attachments, and linked documents, mirrored into your folders on pipeline add.

One explorer for the whole library

Solicitations, past performance, and other directories in a single outline, so nobody hunts through six shared drives for the last proposal.

Security & Trust

Built for controlled information

Government contractors handle other people's sensitive data. Bidmana is engineered so that data stays inside a controlled boundary, isolated by company, and never used for anything but your own work.

Google Cloud Assured Workloads

Bidmana runs inside an Assured Workloads folder with the FedRAMP Moderate control package, which restricts the environment to authorized services, US-only regions, and FIPS-validated encryption.

AI that never leaves the boundary

Every model call runs inside the same cloud boundary. Your solicitations, drafts, and library are never sent to a public AI endpoint and never used to train a model.

Isolation enforced in the database

Company data is separated with row-level security enforced by the database itself, not just the application, and the platform checks that enforcement is active on every start.

Strong sign-in and sessions

Multi-factor authentication, login rate limiting, and company administrator control over every active session.

Scanned uploads, tamper-evident audit

Every file is scanned for malware before it is accepted, and every significant action is written to a hash-chained audit log that cannot be quietly edited.

A data processing agreement with every company

Each customer accepts a data processing agreement before its workspace opens, with export and purge available on request.
