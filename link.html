Bidmana Link

Find the right teaming partners without leaving your proposal workspace

Bidmana Link is Bidmana's network for companies seeking teaming partners and strategic partnerships. Companies opt in and become discoverable by their capabilities, NAICS codes, and certifications, making it easy to find the right partner for any pursuit.

Request a Demo
The Network

The partner you need is already in the room

Whether you are looking for a large prime to team with, a niche subcontractor with a specific capability, or a small business to meet set-aside requirements, Bidmana Link surfaces the right match inside the platform where you are already working.

Every company card is built from the company's own settings and verified SAM.gov registration, so what you see reflects what the company can actually bring to the team.

Opt-in discovery

Companies choose to be listed and control exactly what capabilities, codes, and contacts they advertise to the network.

Capability, NAICS, and certification matching

Search by core competencies, NAICS codes, business size, and certifications such as 8(a), HUBZone, SDVOSB, and WOSB, or by past performance areas.

Engage from the card

Expand a company card to see its points of contact, then open a conversation or a formal teaming engagement in one click. Both companies see the same record.

Integrated Into Your Workflow

Teaming that stays attached to the pursuit

Find partners directly inside Bidmana while you are working a pursuit, not through a separate directory. Conversations, engagements, and shared documents live with the opportunity they support.

One conversation per partner

A single thread between your company and theirs, with email notices to the people who need to know and nothing lost in personal inboxes.

Meetings on your own calendar

Schedule teaming calls from Bidmana. Invites go to both sides, and Teams links are created on your company's own tenant.

Verified, current registrations

Company profiles are checked against SAM.gov entity data, and key registrations and certifications are tracked for expiry so a partner's status never surprises you at submission.

Get listed, get found

Bidmana Link is included with every company workspace. Reach out to see how your company would appear to the primes and partners in your market.
